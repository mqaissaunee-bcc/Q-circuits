Schematic

Pick a part, then click the sheet to place it. Drag on empty space to box-select. Middle-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Alt-drag to peel off a copy. Double-click a value to edit it in place. Pan drags the view; Zoom area frames a rectangle.
